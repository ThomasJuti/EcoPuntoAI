# education Specification

## Purpose

Short educational content so users understand RAEE without leaving the app.

## Requirements

### Requirement: RAEE basics

Aprende MUST briefly explain what WEEE/RAEE are and why they MUST NOT go in household trash. It MUST include special guidance for cells/batteries and for damaged devices.

#### Scenario: User opens Aprende

- GIVEN any visitor
- WHEN they open Aprende
- THEN they see short sections on RAEE, household trash, batteries, and damaged devices
- AND each section is scannable without a long single page of dense text
