# home-onboarding Specification

## Purpose

Short in-app home after the visitor leaves the marketing landing.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Brief purpose on in-app Inicio

The in-app Inicio (`/app`) MUST briefly remind the user that EcoPunto IA identifies an electronic device, says what they can do with it, explains risks, and shows where to take it in Bogotá. The page MUST include a primary call to action to identify a device. This page is not the marketing landing.

#### Scenario: Returning user inside the app

- GIVEN a visitor who already entered `/app`
- WHEN they open Inicio
- THEN they see a short purpose reminder and Identificar
- AND the app tab bar is visible
